Delete your Toli account

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open Toli and tap You.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap More, then Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete my account again, then Yes, delete everything.

Without the app

Email hlo.krsna@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Toli account", from the Google account or with the mobile number you use in Toli. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, picture and linked Google accounts.
  • Your place in every circle. Circles you look after pass to the member who has been there longest; a circle with nobody else in it is closed.
  • The videos you added to circles, their pictures, and your comments, likes and views.
  • Invites you sent, and the app's error logs linked to your account.

What is kept

  • Your video files stay in your own Google Drive. They belong to you; Toli stops sharing them. Delete them in Google Drive if you want them gone too.
