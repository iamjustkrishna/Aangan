Toli

Your people's videos, in one private place

Record or upload a video once. Everyone in your circle, family or friends, can watch it. Each video stays in the uploader's own Google Drive.

Get Toli on Google Play (Android, India).

Only your circle

People join only by invite. There are no public profiles and no ads.

Your own Drive

The app can only see the videos it saves there. The rest of your Drive stays private.

Easy for everyone

Big buttons, a simple feed, and sign in with your mobile number.
