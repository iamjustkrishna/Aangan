Toli

Child safety standards

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Toli is a private video app for families and small groups. People can only see videos in circles they were invited to. This page sets out our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero tolerance

Toli does not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualised images or videos of minors, grooming, sextortion, and any attempt to contact, solicit or traffic a child. This applies to videos, titles, comments, names and profile pictures.

What we do

  • Remove it: when we find or are told about such content, we remove it and the account that posted it, and we ask Google Drive to act on the stored file.
  • Report it: we report child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and Indian law enforcement through the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), as the law requires.
  • Keep evidence: we keep the information the law requires us to keep for investigators, and share it only with them.
  • Private by design: there are no public profiles, no search for strangers and no way to message people outside your circle. Only a circle's admin can invite people.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: tap ⋮ on any video and choose "Report a concern", or go to You → "Report a concern". It opens an email to us with the circle and video already filled in.
  • In your circle: if you are the circle's admin, remove the person from the circle straight away, then email us.
  • By email: write to hlo.krsna@gmail.com with "Child safety" in the subject. Say which circle and video, if you can. We read every report and act quickly.
  • If a child is in danger now: call 112 (India) or your local emergency number. In India you can also call Childline on 1098 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.

Child safety contact

Our point of contact for child safety and for Google Play is hlo.krsna@gmail.com. They can speak to our CSAE prevention practices and compliance.

Laws we follow

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and Information Technology Act, and Google Play's child safety standards.
