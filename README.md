<h1 align="center">Aangan</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Your family's videos, in one private place.</b></p>

Birthdays, first steps, festivals, a grandparent's song. In group chats these videos get buried and compressed. **Aangan** keeps them together, so anyone in the family can open the app and find them.

## What it does

- **Private circles.** Make a circle for your family. Only people the admin invites can join.
- **Invite by mobile number.** No email needed. Sign in with your number and a one-time code.
- **Your own Google Drive.** Videos you add are saved in your own Google Drive. Aangan can only open the videos it saves there.
- **Reels.** Short videos recorded upright play full screen. Swipe up for the next one.
- **Likes and comments,** so the conversation stays with the video.
- **Simple for everyone,** including grandparents. Uploads keep going even if you close the app.
